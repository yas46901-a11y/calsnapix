### 3.2 Contraindication: Eating Disorders
CalSnapix is contraindicated for and not intended for use by individuals with an active diagnosis or historical diagnosis of **anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, binge eating disorder, orthorexia**, or any other disordered eating condition. ### 3.3 Food Allergies & Anaphylaxis Warning
Camera food scanning, barcode recognition, and leftover recipe generators are algorithmic estimates derived from general Indian food composition databases. **They cannot detect invisible traces of allergens, airborne cross-contamination, hidden nuts, dairy derivatives, or specific culinary frying oils.** If you have severe, life-threatening food allergies (such as peanut, tree nut, dairy, gluten, or shellfish anaphylaxis), you must independently examine all physical food packaging and prepare food in sterile environments. ### 3.4 Pregnancy, Lactation & Minors
Nutritional demands during pregnancy and lactation are significantly elevated and dynamic. Caloric restriction regimens are strictly contraindicated during pregnancy and breastfeeding unless explicitly prescribed by an obstetrician.

### 3.5 Chronic Conditions
If you have Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes, hypertension, renal failure, cardiac illness, polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), thyroid disorders, or are taking insulin or blood pressure medications, you must obtain medical clearance before altering your caloric, sodium, or carbohydrate intake.
